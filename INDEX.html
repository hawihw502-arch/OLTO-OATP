Introduction

   

Organizations produce huge amounts of data every day. Some systems are built to record this data quickly and correctly (OLTP), others to analyze it and support decisions (OLAP), and a data warehouse is the central place where historical data from many sources is stored for analysis.

 
 
   

1. OLTP (Online Transaction Processing)

   

OLTP processes daily transactions.

   
Example: ATM money withdrawal.
   

Characteristics

   
         
  • Very fast response for small, simple queries (insert, update, delete).
    •      
  • Supports many users at the same time.
    •      
  • Stores current, detailed data.
    •      
  • Highly normalized database to avoid duplication and keep accuracy.
    •    
   

Typical users

   

Bank tellers, cashiers, booking clerks, customers.

   

More examples

   
         
  • Online shopping orders
    •      
  • Airline ticket reservation
    •      
  • Student registration
